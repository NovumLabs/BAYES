# Research methodology

## Minimum experiment record

A research experiment should record:
- Question and hypothesis.
- Inputs and data provenance.
- Method and configuration.
- Software version and source revision.
- Observations and metrics.
- Verification method.
- Outcome, uncertainty, and limitations.

## Outcome discipline

Use `supported`, `rejected`, `inconclusive`, `failed`, or `needs_review` only when the status is justified by the experiment's defined criteria. Do not force an inconclusive result into a positive or negative conclusion.

## Finite testing versus proof

Finite computational checks can reveal counterexamples and increase confidence within a tested range. They do not establish a universal mathematical theorem unless combined with an appropriate proof method.
