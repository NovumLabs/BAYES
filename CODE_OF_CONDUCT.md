# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment
We aim to make participation in BAYES welcoming, respectful, and constructive.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Explain technical disagreements clearly.
- Credit sources and contributors.
- Report errors, limitations, and negative results honestly.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting
Contact the project maintainers through the repository's issue or contact channels. Do not post sensitive security details in public issues.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that do not follow these expectations.
